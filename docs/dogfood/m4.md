Observations for nomorechat (not blocking):
- The secret masking on the client's stdout replaces single digits that also occur in
  `NMC_GPU_TOKEN`. As a result, the job id, the page ref and even the adapter line printed by
  gpu-capture.mjs come out as `D[REDACTED]D[REDACTED]2 ... RTX [REDACTED][REDACTED]9[REDACTED]`, and the
  numbers in the console log cannot be read. The JSON on disk is unaffected. What nomorechat should do instead:
  mask only the full token (or substrings of length >= 8), never single characters.
- firstFrameMs 469.4 is within 31 ms of the 500 ms limit, compared with 196 ms for m3-prims. This is the
  owner's call and is recorded here so a later regression is not surprising: M4 init loads three assets
  and builds their meshes before the first frame.
