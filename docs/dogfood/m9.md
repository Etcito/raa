# M9 dogfood: GPU client, sandbox and tooling

## m09-shadows-ao scene and capture handoff (card 4600), 2026-10-23

### Producer

What was done:
- `examples/lookdev/scenes/m09-shadows-ao.js`: the m08 `day` sky overrides, IBL diffuse 0.75 (1 since card 4671),
  AgX `punchy`, the candy tile floor, and an obstacle-like set: the inflatable hammer, three
  rounded blocks (a cube stacked on a wide block, a third against its side: creases and inner
  corners), three spheres resting on the floor, five 5-10 cm spheres and cubes, and a
  0.3 x 3 x 0.3 m pillar at the back right. The sun is 35 deg high, behind the set and 50 deg
  to the right, so shadows fall toward the camera and to the left (as in ref-1). Ten
  variants: `all-on` (judged against ref-1 with the m08-style scoring basis), `all-off`, the
  `shadows`, `ao` and `contact` on/off pairs (close cameras on the creases and on the small
  objects), `softness-wide` (softness 3) and `long-shadow` (sun at 8 deg).
- `.nmc/commands.json` for the GPU card: build-lookdev, capture-m09 (`--judge --ref-prefix
  /refs/`, as m08), proof-m9. Nothing was run on the GPU by this card.

Friction met:
- The task text asks for "the 11 variant ids" while it lists ten variants (all-on, all-off,
  three on/off pairs, softness-wide, long-shadow). The listed ten were written; no eleventh
  variant was invented.
- Camera framing, sun direction and placements were chosen without a render (a model card
  cannot run a GPU job), so the first capture is the first view of the framing; the m08
  dogfood asks for a one-variant preview command and for the failing card's PNGs to reach the
  fix card, and both still apply here.
- Only a stub-engine run of `build()` (node, all variants) was possible in the sandbox: it
  checks the geometry calls and the setters called, not the look.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Same as m08: a lookdev software preview command (one variant to PNG) and the capture PNGs
  plus full judge output handed to any fix card.

## Fix of the first m09 GPU card (card 4671), 2026-10-23

What broke:
- `capture-m09` failed only on the `all-on` judge: "shadows are dark grey rather than
  coloured, the horizon is a black stippled band with dither noise across the floor". All
  ten records were hardware and nonEmpty.
- Cause 1, `shadow.contact`: the first march steps stay in the receiver's own pixel, where the
  reconstructed depth equals the origin's, so with the sun behind the set (the ray moves away
  from the camera) `scene.z - p.z` was a tiny positive value below the thickness and nearly
  every pixel shadowed itself, worse with distance (the stippled band toward the horizon).
  Fixed in `contact.wgsl`: samples at pixel centres, the own pixel skipped, and an occluder
  must be in front by more than the receiver's one-pixel depth change.
- Cause 2, `ao.gtao`: both horizons started at the view vector and the "occluded wedge"
  between them always covered it, so a flat surface facing the camera got visibility 0 and
  everything outside the sun lost its ambient light (the dark grey shadows). Rewritten in
  `gtao.wgsl` as the Jimenez 2016 / XeGTAO slice form: horizon cosines from the tangent-plane
  bound, distance falloff, analytic arc integral normalised by the unoccluded slice integral.
- The scene now uses IBL diffuse 1 (was 0.75) so the shadows keep the sky tint of ref-1.

What was done by hand:
- No GPU render was possible from the model card, and the sandbox's SwiftShader Chrome fails
  every `mapAsync` of the engine (`core.gpu: buffer map failed`), so the kernels were checked
  by a CPU port in node on a ray-traced depth buffer of the `wide` and `small` cameras: old
  contact 90-100% of floor pixels shadowed, new 0% on lit faces (only back-lit faces and the
  4 cm sphere's contact); new GTAO about 0.92-0.98 on the open floor with darkening at the
  box base and under the sphere.

What nomorechat must do instead:
- Hand the failing card's capture PNGs to the fix card (the judge text alone had to be
  reverse-engineered into two shader bugs), and offer a software preview path whose WebGPU
  supports buffer mapping.

## Fix of the second m09 GPU card (card 4683), 2026-10-23

What broke:
- `capture-m09` failed only on the `all-on` judge again (all ten records hardware and
  nonEmpty): "no visible soft diagonal sun shadows, no long pillar shadow stripe, no contact
  shadows under the tiny objects ... the scene looks flat".
- A local render showed the cascaded shadows working but unreadable: with sun 5.5 against
  full sky diffuse the shadowed floor was barely darker than the lit floor, the busy 0.5 m
  multicolour tiles hid what remained, the sun 50 deg right of -Z sent the pillar's shadow
  behind the block set (hidden), and the 2 cm gaps between tiles showed the sky below the
  floor as bright seams.
- Fixed in the scene file only: sun 8 with sky diffuse 0.6, sun azimuth 65 deg (shadows
  to the left and toward the camera), the pillar moved to the right foreground so its 4.3 m
  stripe crosses open floor, a grout slab under the tiles, and descriptions to match (the
  5-10 cm objects are only a few pixels wide at the wide camera, so all-on no longer asks
  the judge for thin contact lines under them; contact-on/off keep their close camera).

What was done by hand:
- Local preview: the sandbox Chrome renders the lookdev page with SwiftShader WebGPU,
  including `mapAsync`, when launched with `--enable-unsafe-webgpu --enable-features=Vulkan
  --use-vulkan=swiftshader --use-angle=swiftshader` (without them every map fails with "A
  valid external Instance reference no longer exists" and the canvas reads back empty). A
  small CDP-over-pipe script built the engine, rendered 2 frames and read the canvas through
  a 2D canvas; about 20 s per 1280x720 frame of this scene. Software renders are previews
  only, never records.

What nomorechat must do instead:
- Ship those Chrome flags (or a one-variant software preview command) with the sandbox, and
  hand the failing card's capture PNGs to the fix card.

## Fix of the third m09 GPU card (card 4687), 2026-10-23

What broke:
- `capture-m09` failed only on the `all-on` judge, score 5 (all ten records hardware and
  nonEmpty): "the pillar's long shadow stripe across the foreground is not visible, and the
  render is much flatter and less glossy than the reference".
- A software preview of the committed scene showed why: with the sun 65 deg right of -Z the
  pillar's stripe pointed at the camera (foreshortened), the pillar was cut by the top of the
  frame, and the 0.5 m saturated tiles with dark grout broke every shadow into small pieces;
  all materials were at roughness 0.25-0.55, so nothing reflected the sky.
- Fixed in the scene file only: sun 80 deg right of -Z (shadows run to the left across the
  screen), a 2.2 m pillar in the right foreground fully in frame, the floor built from 1.5 m
  rounded panels (4 colour meshes instanced on a 20 x 20 grid, slightly lighter palette,
  pale seams) instead of `objects.floorTiles`, glossier materials (floor 0.3, blocks 0.15,
  spheres 0.12), a closer wide camera, and descriptions to match.

What was done by hand:
- The same SwiftShader CDP-over-pipe preview as card 4683 (rewritten from scratch, the
  earlier script lived in that card's /tmp): build once, then 3-4 frames per variant, about
  20 s each, for all-on, ao-on, contact-on and long-shadow. Previews only, never records.

What nomorechat must do instead:
- Hand the failing card's capture PNGs to the fix card, and keep a one-variant software
  preview script in the sandbox tooling instead of each card rewriting it.

## Tuning (card 4602), 2026-10-23

Starting point: the `all-on` judge passed at 7.0/10 (minimum 6) with two remarks: "contact
occlusion between the stacked blocks is weak and the flat brown horizon band looks crude".
No capture PNG reached this card, so every value below was chosen on SwiftShader previews of
the lookdev page (previews only, never records).

Values changed, all in `examples/lookdev/scenes/m09-shadows-ao.js`:
- `DAY_SKY.ground` [0.5, 0.45, 0.4] -> [0.45, 0.62, 0.92] (the only deviation from the m08
  `day` sky). Addresses "the flat brown horizon band looks crude": the 30 m floor ends well
  before the horizon, and below the horizon the sky shows its ground colour, so a band of
  flat brown sat between the far panels and the blue horizon. It is now a pale blue haze
  continuing the horizon colour. Side effect: the lower half of the sky irradiance is bluer,
  so vertical faces (the pillar) turn a cooler grey.
- `AO` radius 0.8 -> 1 m, power 1.5 -> 3 (intensity stays 1, its maximum). Addresses
  "contact occlusion between the stacked blocks is weak": at power 1.5 the crease under the
  yellow cube and the inner corner against the pink block were barely darker than the open
  faces; at power 3 the creases and block bases darken clearly in the `ao-on` close view and
  visibly in the wide `all-on` view. The `ao-on`/`ao-off` pair gains contrast as well.
- New `LOW_SUN` for `long-shadow` only: colour [1, 0.93, 0.82] -> [1, 0.8, 0.6], intensity
  8 -> 12. No judge remark (the variant is not judged): at 8 deg the sun put about a quarter
  of the 35 deg irradiance on the floor and the long shadows were faint against the sky
  fill. At 12 the floor still gets about a third of the `all-on` sun irradiance, so the
  description's "darker and warmer" holds. The gain in the preview is modest: warmer light
  and slightly more contrast on the long shadows.

Kept on purpose:
- Shadow softness 1 (`softness-wide` 3, three times wider): the judge praised the "coloured
  soft shadows", and softness 1.5 was hardly distinguishable from 1 in the wide view.
- Contact shadows (distance 0.35, thickness 0.05, intensity 1), the 35 deg sun (intensity
  8), the sky diffuse 0.6, exposure 0.3 EV and the cameras: the judge listed every element as
  present and the mood as matching ref-1.
- `long-shadow`: the pillar stripe already crosses the frame to its left edge, and still
  does with the new sun.
- No `raa-shadow` constant changed (CONSTANT_BIAS 2, SLOPE_BIAS 2.0, NORMAL_OFFSET 1.5
  texels, BLEND_BAND 0.1): neither the judge text nor the previews show acne, peter-panning
  or cascade seams, so the CSM values are kept with no deviation to record.

Capture scope: the two shared values (sky ground, AO) change every variant's image, not only
`all-on`, `softness-wide` and `long-shadow`, so `.nmc/commands.json` recaptures the whole
milestone (`--milestone m09 --judge --ref-prefix /refs/`) instead of the three-variant
`--only` form; otherwise seven committed records would show the old look.

Friction met:
- The preview had to be rebuilt again in /tmp (third card in a row). The sky's SH irradiance
  comes from a GPU readback that lands some frames later: a 3-frame preview still uses the
  flat ambient fallback (a sky diffuse comparison made that way showed no difference), so
  8 frames with 400 ms pauses were used, about 20-25 s per variant.
- In those previews only the first engine created in a page got the sky irradiance: a
  variant rendered after `dispose()` and a new `createEngine` in the same page stayed on the
  flat ambient even after 30 frames, and the same variant alone in a fresh page was sky-lit.
  The comparisons behind the values above all use sky-lit renders, the first engine of their
  page. Not investigated further (outside this card's files); harmless for the captures if
  the GPU client loads the page afresh for every `render`, otherwise records after the first
  could lose the sky light.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the judged card's capture PNGs to the tuning card, and keep a one-variant software
  preview command in the sandbox tooling that loads a fresh page per variant and waits for
  the environment readback.
