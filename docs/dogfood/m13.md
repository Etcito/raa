# M13 dogfood: GPU client, sandbox and tooling

## Entries

## Judge failure of the liquid recapture (card 4944, fix card 5083)

What broke:
- The programmatic card recaptured the particle variants and passed its own judge, but the
  m13 proof reads every committed judge record: the slime and mud records were still the
  first-capture 5/10 verdicts, so `judge` failed (4/6) although the particles had improved.
- The failing card's capture PNGs were not handed to this fix card (again), and the judge
  refuses a model card with `403: A model never grades its own work`, so there was no direct
  way to look at the current slime/mud images or to score an iteration before the GPU card.
- The sandbox Chrome renders WebGPU on SwiftShader but cannot read the canvas back:
  `canvas.toDataURL`, `drawImage(canvas)` and CDP `Page.captureScreenshot` all return a blank
  image even though the engine's `probeNonEmpty` is true. The probe's own readback buffer is
  the only GPU pixels reachable from the page.

What was done by hand:
- A throwaway preview harness in /tmp patched `GPUBuffer.prototype.mapAsync` and
  `getMappedRange` in the page, copied the engine's `raa-web-probe-readback` buffer after the
  probe, and encoded it to PNG through a 2D canvas. It renders one lookdev variant at
  640x360 on SwiftShader in about 1-2 minutes, which was enough to tune the slime and mud
  (foam bands, crown sheet shape, spray size, sky sheen) before the recapture.
- The final tuning is scene/shader only: slime transmission/absorption/foam, mud swell and
  coat, crown geometry, spray counts and the white froth. The probe patch is not committed.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the failing programmatic card's capture PNGs and full judge output to the fix card.
- Ship a lookdev software preview command (one variant to PNG) built on the probe readback,
  so a model card can see a scene without a GPU job.
