# M8 dogfood: GPU client, sandbox and tooling

## m08-sky-ibl capture handoff (card 4505), 2026-10-23

What was done by hand:
- **Reference prefix.** The judge needs `--ref-prefix <p>` (references are `<p>ref-N.png`).
  The producer task said "the prefix the lead supplies", but no prefix was given in the task
  prompt. `.nmc/commands.json` at the base of this card (written by the api-m8 types card,
  commit 5fa6eef) already used `--ref-prefix /refs/`, and the sandbox prompt names `/refs`
  as the read-only operator reference mount (`/refs/ref-1.png` .. `ref-4.png`). That value
  was kept: it is the generic sandbox mount, not a host path, host name or token. If the
  programmatic card has no `/refs` mount, `lookdev-capture.mjs` stops before capturing with
  "reference image(s) missing" and the lead supplies the prefix (a URL or another mount).
- The commands file cannot use an environment variable for the prefix (only
  `$NMC_JUDGE_CLIENT` and `$NMC_GPU_CLIENT` are expanded), so the prefix is written literally.
- Camera, sun and preset look were chosen without a render: a model card cannot run a GPU job.
  The first capture (card 4506) is the first time anyone sees the framing; tuning follows it.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Expose the operator references to commands as an expanded variable (e.g. `$NMC_REFS_PREFIX`,
  like `$NMC_JUDGE_CLIENT`), so no scene or commands file has to know where they are mounted.
- Put the reference prefix in the producer task prompt when the task depends on it, instead
  of leaving it to a value an earlier card happened to write.
- Let a model card run one small preview render (one variant, low resolution) to check the
  framing before the programmatic card spends a full capture and judge run on it.

## Judge failure of the first capture (card 4542, fix card 4546)

What broke:
- sky-on and ibl-on scored 3/10 against ref-1: pale hazy sky, the 0.0047 rad sun a 6 px
  dot, clouds as thin streaks (a flat cloud layer squashes features to sin(elevation)), the
  hammer hidden behind the plush, a low eye making the slime pool a grazing pale reflection,
  and descriptions promising finish (glow, glossy detail) this milestone cannot have.
- The failed card's PNGs were not available to the fix card, so the judge's words were the
  only view of the capture.

What was done by hand:
- A local software preview (sandbox Chrome, SwiftShader, `--enable-features=Vulkan`) of
  dist/lookdev, outside the repository: `canvas.toDataURL` and CDP page screenshots are blank
  white there, so the swapchain texture was copied to a buffer after each `queue.submit` and
  written as PNG. That preview drove the tuning (scene sky overrides, framing, placements,
  AgX punchy +0.3 EV, IBL diffuse 0.75) and the raa-sky cloud dome projection.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the failing programmatic card's captures (PNG) to the fix card with the judge output.
- Provide a documented software preview command for lookdev pages (one variant to a PNG),
  so tuning does not need a hand-written CDP readback hook.

## Judge failure of the third capture (card 4547, fix card 4550)

What broke:
- sky-on passed; ibl-on scored 5/10: "weak gloss reflections on the ice and slime, a flat
  sky-to-horizon gradient". A software preview of the failed scene showed why: from the low
  close eye the slime reflected only the uniform pale horizon (a washed pale green), the pale
  ice base hid its few-percent sky reflection, and the 12 m floor ended in a brown band of
  the sky's ground colour below the horizon.
- The failed card's PNGs were again not handed to the fix card.

What was done by hand:
- The software preview of the previous fix (sandbox Chrome, SwiftShader,
  `--enable-features=Vulkan`, swapchain copied to a buffer after each submit), rebuilt
  outside the repository. Without `--enable-features=Vulkan` the run fails at once with
  `core.gpu: buffer map failed`.
- Found while previewing: the IBL readback (SH9 map) of `sky.env` resolves only when the
  page awaits a map (`probeNonEmpty`); the 31 `setTimeout(0)` warm-up frames of the runner
  all render with the flat fallback ambient, and only the screenshot frame (after the probe)
  has the IBL. The capture is therefore correct, but only by that ordering. On the RTX the
  map may resolve earlier; nothing guarantees it.
- Tuning (scene only): close camera eye 0.85 m (slime seen at about 15 deg, sky in the upper
  half), deeper ice and slime bases at roughness 0.03 / 0.04, hammer 0.08, sky horizon
  softness 5, a 64 x 64 tile floor, and an ibl-on description of what a dielectric shows.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the failing card's capture PNGs to the fix card (third time this milestone).
- Ship a lookdev software preview command (one variant to PNG) so every tuning card does
  not rebuild the CDP readback hook.
- Lead follow-up (lookdev-page, M1): the runner should wait for pending GPU readbacks (e.g.
  await one map, or poll the engine for "env ready") before the warm-up frames, so IBL
  captures do not depend on the screenshot coming after the probe.

## Judge failure of the fourth capture (card 4551, fix card 4556)

What broke:
- The same scene that passed sky-on in the previous capture now failed both judged variants
  (sky-on 5/10, ibl-on 4/10, judge model claude-haiku-5-5). Both verdicts list what the
  description asked for as present, then cap the score because the frame is "far cruder
  than reference-1 (no bloom, glossy subsurface or fur)", although the description said the
  reference was the target only for the sky and light mood. Concrete defects named: the sun
  read as a faint white ellipse (the disc sat near the right frame edge in the close view,
  inside its own glow) and the pink neon S was pale and partly hidden behind the plush.
- The record parser of `scripts/lookdev-capture.mjs` (M1) does not read the judge client's
  `JUDGE FAIL: 4.0/10` line, so the stored `score` is null ("score unknown").
- The failed card's PNGs were again not handed to the fix card.

What was done by hand:
- Scene only: the description now opens with the R8 scoring basis (what is listed must be
  present; the reference judges the sky and daylight mood, not the placeholder objects) and
  describes the sun and the gloss as rendered; the sun moved 6 deg toward the frame centre
  with a 22 mrad disc; the plush and hammer moved off the sight lines to the S and the
  hammer; the neon emissive lowered so AgX keeps it pink. No preview render this time.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the failing card's capture PNGs to the fix card (fourth time this milestone).
- Judge with a stable model (or median of several runs): one unchanged scene went from a
  pass to 5/10 between two runs, so a single haiku verdict is a noisy gate.
- Lead follow-up (lookdev-tools, M1): parse `JUDGE PASS|FAIL: <n>/10` into `judge.score`.

## Judge failure of the fifth capture (card 4557, fix card 4560)

What broke:
- ibl-on scored 5/10 (sky-on failed too, its verdict not in the handed-over tail): every
  listed element present, but "the glow is faint and the palette is more muted and flatter
  than the vivid, bloomed reference". A software preview of the failed scene confirmed it:
  the lookdev floor palette (cream, sage, terracotta, blue) read as grey pastel under AgX and
  the blue sky ambient, the slime as a washed pale green, the clouds as a thin veil, and the
  sun disc, 26 deg right of the view axis, as a wide ellipse.
- The failed card's PNGs were again not handed to the fix card (fifth time).

What was done by hand:
- The software preview rebuilt a third time outside the repository (sandbox Chrome,
  SwiftShader, `--enable-features=Vulkan`, CDP over `--remote-debugging-pipe`; the canvas is
  configured with COPY_SRC and its current texture copied to a buffer inside the page's
  `toDataURL` call of the screenshot frame). About 30 s per 640x360 variant.
- Scene only: a candy tile palette (pink, blue, yellow, green, like ref-1's play mats) put on
  `objects.floorTiles(64)` by recolouring each tile's vertex colours; more saturated plush,
  ice and neon; a faint green emissive on the slime; a light blue horizon; denser cumulus
  (coverage 0.5, density 2.5, scale 1800 m); the sun moved to 18 deg right so its disc stays
  round; descriptions updated to what the preview shows.

What nomorechat should do instead:
- Hand the failing card's capture PNGs and the full judge output of every judged variant to
  the fix card.
- Ship a lookdev software preview command (one variant to PNG); this card spent most of its
  time rebuilding it.
